About Us

Uqentra - Revolutionizing Trading for All

Uqentra is a modern trading and investment platform powered by Uqentra USA. We focus primarily on cryptocurrency, stocks, and indices, with forex, and commodities becoming available soon to offer even more options. Our mission is to create a secure, easy-to-use, and well-rounded environment where both individual traders and institutions can participate in various markets confidently.

We designed the platform to make trading and investing more accessible to everyone. It quickly became popular by allowing users to trade stocks, indices, options, and cryptocurrencies with no commission fees, moving away from traditional brokers that charge for every trade.

History

A New Era in Finance

Uqentra USA launched in 2012 as a niche data tool before pivoting to a mobile-first brokerage designed to strip away the gatekeeping of traditional finance. By removing commission fees and simplifying the user experience, it became a primary gateway for a new generation of retail investors. The platform reached a cultural boiling point in 2021 during the GameStop short squeeze, where it saw record-breaking traffic but also faced intense backlash for temporarily halting trades on volatile stocks.

Following a period of regulatory scrutiny and public debate over market transparency, the company overhauled its infrastructure and expanded into a "financial super-app." Today, Uqentra integrates everything from stock trading to AI-driven wealth management, maintaining its position as a major, albeit controversial, force in the democratization of the American markets.
