British Pound / U.S. Dollar Trading (GBP/USD)

GBP/USD, commonly known as "The Cable," is one of the oldest and most liquid currency pairs in the Forex market, representing the exchange rate between the British Pound Sterling and the U.S. Dollar. As a cornerstone of global finance, its price action is a real-time reflection of the relative economic strength between the United Kingdom and the United States. Unlike fixed assets, the GBP/USD exchange rate is highly sensitive to interest rate decisions from the Bank of England (BoE) and the Federal Reserve, as well as geopolitical events and macroeconomic data like GDP and inflation reports.
